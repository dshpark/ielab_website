About the conference

KDI School of Public Policy and Management and World Bank's Development Impact Group (DECDI) are pleased to host the 7th Development Impact Conference in Seoul, Korea. The conference brings together researchers presenting rigorous impact evaluation work across the full range of development topics.

The call for papers closed on June 21, 2026.

Keynote speaker
Johannes Haushofer
Professor of Economics and Public Affairs, Cornell University

His research spans development and behavioral economics. He is the founder of Busara, a non-profit advancing behavioral science for poverty alleviation, and Malengo, an organization facilitating international migration. He holds a BA from Oxford, a PhD in Neurobiology from Harvard, and a PhD in Economics from Zurich.
