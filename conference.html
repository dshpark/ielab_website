Development Impact Conference

An annual research conference co-hosted by KDI School of Public Policy and Management and World Bank's Development Impact Group (DECDI, formerly DIME). Since 2020, the conference has convened leading impact evaluation researchers, alternating between Seoul and Washington, D.C. — spanning agriculture, climate, education, health, governance, labor, finance, and gender.
